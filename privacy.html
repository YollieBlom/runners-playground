Privacy policy

Your runs, your details, your choice. Here's exactly what The Runners Playground collects, why, where it's kept and how to have it removed. Written in plain language, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

The short version

  • Most of the Playground works without giving us anything. Your Run Diary, race predictions and settings stay on your own phone or computer. We never see them.
  • We only collect details when you choose to: joining the newsletter, adding yourself to a race leaderboard, requesting a race route, or switching on the daily nudge.
  • We never sell your information, and we don't use advertising trackers. Sponsor banners are simple links that don't track you.
  • You can ask us to see, fix or delete your information at any time.

Who we are

The Runners Playground (runnersplayground.com, runnersplayground.co.za and pacer.runnersplayground.com) is a free running website built and run by Yolandi Jansen van Vuuren in Mpumalanga, South Africa. Under POPIA, we are the "responsible party" for your information, and Yolandi is our Information Officer.

Questions or requests? Email hello@runnersplayground.com.

Kept on your device only (we never receive it)

  • Run Diary and Heat Analyzer: your runs, username and PIN. Your diary is locked with your PIN and saved in your browser.
  • Race prediction: the recent runs you type in, and your pacer settings (goal, weight, cadence, strategy).
  • Small preferences: for example that you've joined the newsletter, your supporter code, or which tab you last opened.

Clearing your browser data deletes these. Because they never leave your device, we can't recover them for you.

Things that happen automatically

  • Weather: when you use the Heat Analyzer, the town name you type is sent to Open-Meteo to look up the weather. Race pacers ask for the weather at the race start, never at your location.
  • Hosting and fonts: like any website, the servers that deliver our pages (GitHub Pages) and our fonts (Google Fonts) receive your IP address and browser details so they can send you the page. We don't use these to identify you.
  • No advertising cookies or tracking. We don't use Google Analytics, Facebook pixels or similar trackers.

Where your information is kept

We use a few trusted services to run the Playground. Some of their servers are outside South Africa (mainly in the USA and Europe). POPIA allows this when the service protects your information to a standard similar to South Africa's, which these services commit to in their own privacy terms.

  • Google (Google Sheets and Google Drive): newsletter list, leaderboards, route requests and game scores.
  • OneSignal (USA): daily nudge subscriptions and reminder settings.
  • GitHub (USA): hosts the website itself. It doesn't store your form details.
  • Payment service (for Supporters): handles your payment securely. We only receive a confirmation.

How long we keep it

  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe. After that we keep only a note that you unsubscribed, so we never email you again by mistake.
  • Race leaderboards: kept as race history so runners can look back at past races, unless you ask us to remove your entry.
  • Route requests: deleted within 12 months, or sooner once the pacer is built.
  • Daily nudge: until you tap "Turn off" or block notifications for our site.
  • Supporters: payment records are kept for as long as the law requires for financial records (usually 5 years).

Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • ask what information we hold about you, and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct it or delete it;
  • object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent at any time (for example by unsubscribing or turning off the nudge);
  • complain to the Information Regulator (see below).

To do any of these, email hello@runnersplayground.com. We'll reply as soon as we can, usually within a week. We may ask you to confirm it's really you before we share or change anything.

Young runners

Runners of all ages are welcome to use the Playground's tools. If you're under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before you join the newsletter, add your name to a leaderboard or request a route. They can contact us at any time to see or remove your details.

Keeping it safe

Our accounts are protected with strong passwords and two-step verification, only we have access to the spreadsheets, and the website runs on secure HTTPS connections. Your Run Diary is locked with your own PIN. No system is perfect, though: if we ever discover a breach that affects your information, we'll tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

Changes to this policy

We're still building the Playground. When we add something new that uses personal information (for example optional log-in accounts, which are coming soon), we'll update this page before it goes live and change the date at the top.

Not happy?

Please talk to us first at hello@runnersplayground.com, and we'll do our best to put it right.

You can also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za · email POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za · tel 010 023 5200.
